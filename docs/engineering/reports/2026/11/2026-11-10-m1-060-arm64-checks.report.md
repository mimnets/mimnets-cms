## Change

**`.github/workflows/staging-arm64.yml`:**

- The kernel step writes `uname -r` as a notice annotation and in the step summary.
- The three images are saved with `docker save` and uploaded as a one-day artifact,
  `staging-arm64-images`. **New action: `actions/upload-artifact` v7.0.2, pinned to `cf430e0`**, added
  with the owner's scan choice. The images hold no secrets: the stack makes them at first start.
- After the restart: log in as the stack's own admin (password and token masked, kept on the runner),
  upload 5 MB through nginx, compare by SHA-256 with what nginx serves back.
- A marker page is published through the REST API after the second base backup, which the restart
  starts. Only an oplog replay can bring it back.
- The run waits until a PITR range covers the marker, then follows PBM runbook §3 steps 2 and 3 in the
  `restore` service, and checks the marker page and the uploaded file on the restored site.
- Diagnostics include the restore container. Clean-up uses `--profile restore down -v`. Timeout 45 → 60
  minutes.

**Records:** this report, the prompt, the staging and PBM runbooks (§7 and §8; §3 and §7), a D-SEC-8
